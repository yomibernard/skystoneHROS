# Requirements

The Business Requirements Document (BRD) is maintained as a live document:
https://claude.ai/artifact/JZKJ2ujerWjRDjtcjZZSWk

It covers the ten HR pillars, integrations, Nigerian regulatory requirements, non-functional requirements, roles and approvals, delivery options, the roadmap, and **Annex A: Security, resilience and assurance requirements (SEC-01 to SEC-25)**.

To keep a copy in this repository, open the BRD, choose **Export → Markdown**, and save it here as `docs/BRD.md`.

The capability map and test log are kept in the companion spreadsheet:
https://claude.ai/artifact/VGPpmgYujWu8BUtp3xYbrA
